Basic Definitions

Probability: a numerical measure (between 0 and 1) of the likelihood that an event will occur.
Random experiment: a process that produces outcomes where the exact result cannot be predicted with certainty before the experiment.
Sample space (S): the set of all possible outcomes of an experiment.
Event: any subset of the sample space (denoted A, B, C, …).

الاحتمال مقياس بين 0 و1 لإمكانية وقوع حدث. التجربة العشوائية نتيجتها غير معروفة مسبقاً. فضاء العينة S مجموعة كل النتائج الممكنة. الحدث أي مجموعة جزئية من S.

Finding the Sample Space

S = {all distinct possible outcomes}.
Method: (1) identify all possible individual outcomes, (2) list them without duplication, (3) verify completeness (no possible outcome is missing).
Examples: one toss S={H,T}; two tosses S={HH,HT,TH,TT}; until first head S={H,TH,TTH,TTTH,…}; one die S={1,…,6}; two dice S={(1,1),(1,2),…,(6,6)} with 36 outcomes; baby’s gender; true/false.

خطوات إيجاد فضاء العينة: حدد كل النتائج، اكتبها بدون تكرار، وتأكد أنك لم تنس أي نتيجة.

Key properties & common mistakes

Key properties: exhaustive (must cover all possibilities), mutually exclusive (outcomes shouldn’t overlap), finite or infinite (depends on the experiment).
Visual for two coins: S = {HH, HT, TH, TT}.
Common mistakes: omitting possible outcomes; including impossible outcomes; counting outcomes multiple times; confusing events with sample spaces.

الفضاء شامل ومتنافي النتائج وقد يكون محدوداً أو لا نهائياً. أخطاء شائعة: نسيان نتائج، إضافة نتائج مستحيلة، تكرار العدّ، الخلط بين الحدث وفضاء العينة.

Probability Rules

For any event A: P(A) = n(A)/n(S) = favorable outcomes / total outcomes.

  1. 0 ≤ P(A) ≤ 1 for any event A.
  2. P(A) = Σ P(e) over the simple events e in A.
  3. P(S) = 1 and P(∅) = 0.

P(A)=عدد النتائج المناسبة ÷ عدد كل النتائج. الاحتمال بين 0 و1، واحتمال الحدث = مجموع احتمالات نتائجه البسيطة، P(S)=1 و P(∅)=0.

Formulas

P(A) = n(A) / n(S)0 ≤ P(A) ≤ 1P(S) = 1 , P(∅) = 0P(A) = Σ P(e) , e ∈ ATwo dice: n(S) = 36

Examples with full solutions (6)

Tap each question to reveal the solution.

Ex 1. Toss a coin twice. S = {HH,HT,TH,TT}. Find P(exactly one head), P(at least one head), P(no heads), P(two heads)

n(S)=4.
Exactly one head: {HT,TH} → 2/4 = 0.5.
At least one head: {HH,HT,TH} → 3/4 = 0.75.
No heads: {TT} → 1/4 = 0.25.
Two heads: {HH} → 1/4 = 0.25.

Ex 2. Roll a die. Find P(2), P(odd), P(negative number), P(greater than 3), P(≥ 2)

n(S)=6.
P(2) = 1/6 ≈ 0.1667. P({1,3,5}) = 3/6 = 0.5.
Negative number: impossible event, P(∅) = 0.
P({4,5,6}) = 3/6 = 0.5. P({2,3,4,5,6}) = 5/6 ≈ 0.8333.

Ex 3. S = {e₁, e₂, e₃} with P(e₁)=0.3, P(e₂)=0.5. Find P(e₃)

P(S) = 1 → 0.3 + 0.5 + P(e₃) = 1 → P(e₃) = 1 − 0.8 = 0.2.

Ex 4. Practice 1 Baby’s gender experiment: P(male baby)? P(either male or female)?

Assuming equally likely outcomes: P(Male) = 1/2. P(Male or Female) = P(S) = 1.

Ex 5. Practice 2 S = {a,b,c,d} with P(a)=0.2, P(b)=0.3, P(c)=0.4. Find P(d), P({a,c}), and P(an event with exactly 2 outcomes)

P(d) = 1 − 0.9 = 0.1. P({a,c}) = 0.2 + 0.4 = 0.6.
Exactly two outcomes depends on which pair: {a,b}=0.5, {a,c}=0.6, {a,d}=0.3, {b,c}=0.7, {b,d}=0.4, {c,d}=0.5.

Ex 6. Practice 3 Rolling two dice: P(sum = 7), P(both even), P(at least one 6)

n(S)=36.
Sum 7: (1,6),(2,5),(3,4),(4,3),(5,2),(6,1) → 6/36 = 1/6.
Both even: 3·3 = 9 → 9/36 = 1/4.
At least one 6: 36 − 25 (no six) = 11 → 11/36.

★ Key insight
Always define S first and count n(S). P(A) = n(A)/n(S) works only when all outcomes are equally likely. Try the simulator: with more trials the relative frequency approaches the theoretical probability.
عرّف فضاء العينة أولاً. القانون n(A)/n(S) يصلح فقط للنتائج متساوية الاحتمال. جرّب المحاكي: كلما زادت المحاولات اقترب التكرار النسبي من الاحتمال النظري.
