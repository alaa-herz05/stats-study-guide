What are counting rules?

Methods that describe the total number of ways (or choices) of a certain selection. In probability we use them to get the number of outcomes in a sample space or in an event. We use: the fundamental (product) rule, the factorial, the permutation rule and the combination rule.

قواعد العدّ طرق لإيجاد عدد الطرق الكلي لاختيار معين، ونستخدمها لإيجاد عدد عناصر فضاء العينة أو الحدث.

Rule 1 · Product rule (fundamental counting rule)

If we have events Aᵢ each containing nᵢ outcomes (i = 1,…,k), the number of ways to choose one outcome from each event is n₁·n₂·…·nₖ.
Examples: die then coin → 6×2 = 12 outcomes; 3 roads A→B and 5 roads B→C → 3×5 = 15 ways; 6 shirts and 4 pants → 6×4 = 24 ways.

قاعدة الضرب: عدد الطرق = حاصل ضرب عدد الخيارات في كل مرحلة.

Rule 2 · n-Factorial

n distinct objects drawn sequentially / arranged in a row (order matters, without replacement) can be arranged in n! = n(n−1)(n−2)…3·2·1 ways. With repeated objects (k₁ identical of one kind, k₂ of another, …): n!/(k₁! k₂! … kᵣ!).

ترتيب n شيئاً مختلفاً في صف: n! ، ومع وجود عناصر متكررة نقسم على مضروب عدد كل نوع متكرر.

Rule 3 · Permutation (order matters)

Number of ways of selecting m objects from n distinct objects where order is important:
Pⁿₘ = n!/(n−m)! if selection is without replacement; nᵐ if with replacement. Note: Pⁿₙ = n! and Pⁿ₁ = n.

التباديل (الترتيب مهم): بدون إرجاع n!/(n−m)! ومع الإرجاع nᵐ.

Rule 4 · Combination (order does not matter)

Cⁿₘ = n!/(m!(n−m)!) if selection is without replacement; C(n+m−1, m) = (n+m−1)!/(m!(n−1)!) if with replacement.
Word rules: and = multiply, or = add. ‘At most one’ = zero or one.

التوافيق (الترتيب غير مهم). كلمة (و) تعني ضرب، وكلمة (أو) تعني جمع. (على الأكثر واحد) = صفر أو واحد.

Formulas

n₁·n₂·…·nₖn! = n(n−1)…2·1n!/(k₁!k₂!…kᵣ!)Pⁿₘ = n!/(n−m)! | nᵐ (with repl.)Cⁿₘ = n!/(m!(n−m)!) | C(n+m−1,m) (with repl.)P(event) = n(A)/n(S)

Examples with full solutions (20)

Tap each question to reveal the solution.

Ex 1. Employee IDs have 4 digits. How many numbers if repetition is (1) allowed (2) not allowed?

(1) 10×10×10×10 = 10⁴ = 10000.
(2) 10×9×8×7 = 5040 (the file shows only part 1).

Ex 2. In how many ways can you arrange the letters A, B, C?

3! = 3×2×1 = 6: ABC, ACB, BAC, BCA, CAB, CBA.

Ex 3. In how many ways can 3 boys and 2 girls form a line?

5! = 5×4×3×2×1 = 120.

Ex 4. Arrange the letters A, A, B, C

4!/2! = 12.

Ex 5. Arrange the letters of MISSISSIPPI

1 M, 4 I, 4 S, 2 P = 11 letters: 11!/(4!·4!·2!) = 34650.

Ex 6. HW Arrange the letters of STATISTICS

S×3, T×3, A×1, I×2, C×1 = 10 letters: 10!/(3!·3!·2!) = 3628800/72 = 50400.

Ex 7. Arrange 1, 5, 7 into 3-digit numbers. P(number > 400)?

3! = 6 numbers. Greater than 400: first digit 5 or 7 → 2×2×1 = 4. P = 4/6 = 2/3.

Ex 8. How many words/numbers by rearranging: (1) ABCD (2) ABBA (3) MEMBER (4) 1112235

(1) 4! = 24.
(2) 4!/(2!2!) = 6.
(3) 2 M, 2 E, B, R: 6!/(2!2!) = 180.
(4) 7!/(3!·2!) = 420.

Ex 9. Choose 3 of the letters A,B,C,D,E with order important, (1) without (2) with replacement

(1) P(5,3) = 5!/2! = 5×4×3 = 60.
(2) 5³ = 125.

Ex 10. A school music director selects 2 of 9 plays: one for fall, one for spring. How many possibilities?

Order matters (fall vs spring): P(9,2) = 9×8 = 72.

Ex 11. 4-letter codes from the letters of PENCIL (6 letters): (1) no repetition (2) repetition allowed

(1) P(6,4) = 6×5×4×3 = 360.
(2) 6⁴ = 1296.

Ex 12. Chairperson and assistant chairperson from seven scientists

P(7,2) = 7×6 = 42.

Ex 13. Choose 3 of A,B,C,D,E, order not important, (1) without (2) with replacement

(1) C(5,3) = 10.
(2) C(5+3−1, 3) = C(7,3) = 35.

Ex 14. An advisor chooses 4 students from a class of 12 for the same task

C(12,4) = 495.

Ex 15. Choose 3 books from 7 distinct books, no replacement, order not important

C(7,3) = 35.

Ex 16. A committee of 6 people from 10

C(10,6) = 210.

Ex 17. Club: 7 women, 5 men. Committee of 3 women AND 2 men

C(7,3)·C(5,2) = 35×10 = 350.

Ex 18. 6 men and 7 women. Committee of 5 with at most one woman

Zero OR one woman: C(7,0)C(6,5) + C(7,1)C(6,4) = 1×6 + 7×15 = 111.

Ex 19. Parent-teacher committee of 4 from 20 parents and 5 teachers. P(all teachers)? P(2 teachers and 2 parents)?

n(S) = C(25,4) = 12650.
All teachers: C(5,4)·C(20,0)/C(25,4) = 5/12650 = 1/2530.
2 and 2: C(5,2)·C(20,2)/C(25,4) = 10×190/12650 = 38/253.

Ex 20. Quick examples: 3 dice rolled; 3 parks, 4 restaurants, 6 movies; 5 books on a shelf; 3 of 5 books ordered; 3 of 5 books chosen

3 dice: 6³ = 216. Park–restaurant–movie: 3×4×6 = 72. 5 books: 5! = 120. Ordered 3 of 5: P(5,3) = 60. Chosen 3 of 5: C(5,3) = 10.

★ Key insight
Ask two questions: does ORDER matter? (yes → permutation, no → combination) and is REPETITION allowed? ‘And’ → multiply, ‘or’ → add.
اسأل: هل الترتيب مهم؟ (نعم: تباديل، لا: توافيق) وهل يوجد إرجاع؟ و(و) = ضرب، و(أو) = جمع.
